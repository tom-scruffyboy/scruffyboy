the job

what the work involves.

  • Use the products on your dog the way you actually would: after the walk, after the swim, on the way home. Not staged.
  • Send photos or clips of the mess and the fix. Phone footage is the point; we are not after studio work.
  • Tell us straight when something doesn't work. That is the entire reason the department exists.
  • Let us use your dog's photos on our socials and site, credited to you.
the terms

what the department provides.

  • The kit, free. The dirty dog box, sent out before anyone can buy it. Yours to keep, however the testing goes.
  • Early access. You test before launch, and what you tell us changes what ships.
  • Founder pricing, locked. The first-500 rate on everything after that, permanently. Refills included.
  • Your dog on the roster. A place on the department wall and an introduction post on our socials, linked to your handle.
  • A direct line. You talk to the people making the thing, not a support inbox.
  • No salary. It is an unpaid position at a company that doesn't exist yet. The dog is compensated in product.

before you ask

questions from applicants.

do i get free product?

Yes: the dirty dog box, free, before launch, yours to keep whatever you make of it. After that you keep founder pricing on everything, permanently. What we ask in return is honesty and a few photos, not a review.

does my dog need a following?

No. We are choosing for mess, coat variety and location, not reach. A muddy dog with 40 followers is more useful to us than a clean one with 40,000.

how much work is it?

A few photos a month and an honest opinion. If it starts feeling like a job, you are doing more than we asked.

when do you decide?

We review as applications come in and fill the first twenty before launch. If you are in, you hear from us directly. If the intake is full, you stay on file for the next one.
